---

## 📖 Overview

Habib Engineering is a trusted name in the power and engineering sector of Pakistan. This repository contains the complete source code for their official corporate website, built to showcase their extensive product range, project portfolio, certifications, and industry expertise.

The website is a **full-stack application** featuring a dynamic, database-driven frontend and a secure admin dashboard for complete content management.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🌐 Public Website
- **Modern Landing Page:** Hero section, featured products, trusted clients, and industries served.
- **Dynamic Product Catalog:** Browse products by category with detailed descriptions, specifications, and images.
- **Project Portfolio:** Showcase of completed projects across healthcare, defense, and infrastructure sectors with detailed case studies.
- **Service Pages:** Detailed information on turnkey solutions, MEP, solar, testing & commissioning, and after-sales support.
- **Industries We Serve:** Dedicated pages for Oil & Gas, Power, Healthcare, Infrastructure, Industrial, and Education sectors.
- **Client Showcase:** Display of trusted government and defense clients (MES, FWO, CMH, etc.).
- **Certifications Page:** Showcase of ISO certifications and PEC registrations.
- **Blog System:** Company news, insights, and technical articles.
- **Downloads Center:** Downloadable company profiles, catalogs, and brochures.
- **Request a Quote:** A comprehensive quote request form with dynamic item management and email notifications.
- **Contact Page:** Contact form, Google Maps integration, and office/factory details.
- **SEO Optimized:** Dynamic metadata, sitemaps, robots.txt, and JSON-LD schema markup.

### 🔐 Admin Dashboard (CMS)
- **Secure Authentication:** Supabase Auth with protected routes and role-based access.
- **Dashboard Overview:** Real-time stats and analytics (total products, projects, clients, inquiries).
- **Product Management:** Full CRUD operations with image uploads and category assignment.
- **Category Management:** Manage product categories with images.
- **Project Management:** Add, edit, and delete projects with case study details.
- **Client Management:** Manage the client list with logos and sector info.
- **Blog Management:** Write and publish blog posts with a rich text editor.
- **Certifications Management:** Upload and manage ISO certificates and other credentials.
- **Inquiry Management:** View, read, and respond to contact form submissions and quote requests.
- **Media Library:** Centralized image management.
- **Settings Page:** Manage company phone numbers, emails, addresses, and social media links (dynamic).
- **Real-time Notifications:** Get notified of new inquiries and messages.
